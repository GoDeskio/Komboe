Web vault

Sign in

Check the SHA-256 checksum of a download before installing. Your self-hosted server never contacts Komboe, and outside services stay off unless you turn them on or run a check. Set your server URL in the apps when you first sign in. The switch list is in docs/RUNNING.md in the Komboe source.

Komboe assistant

Asks your Komboe agent after you sign in. It can explain a feature, suggest next steps, or generate a password. It never reads vault items.
