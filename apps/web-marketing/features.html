Features

Everything you need to keep credentials safe and close at hand: 102 features, each credited to the projects that inspired it.

Komboe is pre-alpha: this is the planned feature scope; not every feature is built or verified yet.

Highlights

🔐

Encrypted vault

Passwords, notes, cards and identities, encrypted on your device with Encryptoglyph before anything is synced.

🧩

Browser extension

Autofill and save logins in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave and Safari.

🤖

Android

Native autofill service and biometric unlock on your phone.

🖥️

Desktop

Apps for Windows, macOS and Linux with system-tray quick access.

🌐

Web vault

Reach your vault from any modern browser — decryption still happens locally.

🪪

Passkeys

Create, store and sign in with passkeys alongside your passwords.

🤝

Secure sharing

Share items or collections with family and teammates, end-to-end encrypted.

✨

AI agent integration

Give AI agents scoped, revocable access to specific secrets through an MCP server — never your whole vault.

Built for AI agents, safely

Komboe ships an MCP server so your AI agents can request only the secrets you approve. Access is scoped per agent, logged, and revocable at any time. Secrets can also be injected into processes with komboe-run references like komboe://item/field.

$ komboe-run --env DB_PASS=komboe://db/password -- ./deploy.sh
