Features

Everything you need to keep credentials safe and close at hand: 102 features, each with the other products that offer the same idea.

Komboe is pre-alpha. This is the planned feature scope.

Highlights

🔐

Encrypted vault

Passwords, notes, cards and identities are encrypted on your device before anything syncs. The server stores ciphertext only.

Also offered by Bitwarden

🧩

Browser extension

Autofill and save logins from the page you are on, with the same Komboe account as the vault.

Also offered by Bitwarden

🤖

Android

A native autofill service and biometric unlock on your phone, using the same account as every other Komboe app.

Also offered by Bitwarden

🖥️

Desktop

Apps for the major desktop systems, with a tray for quick access and the same vault you use on the web.

Also offered by KeePassXC

🌐

Web vault

Open the vault in a modern browser. Decryption still happens on the device after you sign in.

Also offered by Vaultwarden

🪪

Passkeys

Create, store and sign in with passkeys next to your passwords. The private key never leaves the device.

Also offered by Bitwarden

🤝

Secure sharing

Share an item or a collection with family and teammates. The server never sees the shared secret.

Also offered by Passbolt

✨

AI agent

A signed-in assistant can explain a feature, suggest next steps, or generate a password. It cannot read vault items.

Also offered by the Komboe repository
