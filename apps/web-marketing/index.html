Accounts & sign-in

Do I need to set up any identity server?

No. Sign-in runs on GoDesk's servers and stays invisible to you — you never need to set up any identity server.

How can I sign up?

With email and password, with Google, or with a passkey.

Does one account work everywhere?

Yes. One GoDesk account signs you in to Komboe and every other GoDesk tool.

Can GoDesk or an admin see or reset my master password?

No. Your master password never leaves your device. Komboe is zero-knowledge, and there is no admin reset.

What about self-hosting?

Self-hosted servers can use plain local accounts or bring their own identity provider — Keycloak, Okta, or any OIDC provider. No specific provider is required.
