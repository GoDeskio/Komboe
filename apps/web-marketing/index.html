Accounts & sign-in FAQ

Do I need to set up any identity server?

No. Sign-in is hosted by us at komboe.com and just works — you never need to set up any identity server.

How can I sign up?

Click Sign up and you'll continue on the secure Komboe sign-in page, where you can use email and password, Google, or a passkey. You never type a password on komboe.com.

Does my account work in other apps?

Yes. Your Komboe account works across our other apps too.

Can Komboe or an admin see or reset my master password?

No. Your master password never leaves your device. Komboe is zero-knowledge, and there is no admin reset.

What about self-hosting?

Self-hosted servers can use plain local accounts or bring their own identity provider — Keycloak, Okta, or any OIDC provider. No specific provider is required.
