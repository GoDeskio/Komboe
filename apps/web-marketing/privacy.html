The short version

Komboe is a zero-knowledge password manager. Your vault is encrypted on your device with Encryptoglyph before it is synced. We store only ciphertext. Your master password never leaves your device, so we cannot read your vault, and nobody, including our admins, can reset your master password or recover your data without it.

What we can see

  • Account data: your email address, account settings, plan, and whether a passkey or two-factor authentication is enabled.
  • Encrypted vault data: stored and synced, but unreadable to us.
  • Operational data: limited technical logs (for example timestamps, device type and IP address) used for security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting, kept for [RETENTION PERIOD].
  • Billing data for paid plans, handled by [PAYMENT PROCESSOR]; we do not store full card numbers.

What we cannot see

Your master password, your vault contents (passwords, notes, cards, passkeys, files) and your encryption keys.

This website

How we use data

To run and secure the service, sync your devices, provide support, process payments and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data or use your data for advertising.

Sharing

Only with service providers needed to run Komboe (listed at [SUBPROCESSOR LIST]) or where the law requires. Anything we could be compelled to disclose is limited to account and operational data; vault data stays encrypted.

Your choices

You can export your vault, update your email, or delete your account. Deleting your account removes your encrypted vault from our servers within [PERIOD]. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data; contact us to exercise them.

Self-hosting

If you run your own Komboe server, that server's operator, not us, controls the data on it.

Changes

We will post updates here and notify account holders of material changes.
