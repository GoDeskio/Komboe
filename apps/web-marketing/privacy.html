How your vault is protected

Your vault is encrypted and decrypted on your device. Our servers store your vault and your encryption keys only in encrypted form, plus the limited account, technical and billing data listed below. We never receive your master password in plaintext and cannot decrypt your vault. If you lose your master password (and any recovery method you set up), we cannot recover your vault or reset your password. Your data will be permanently inaccessible.

Self-hosting

If you run your own Komboe server, that server's operator, not us, controls the data on it. Data from our hosted service is governed by this policy. A self-hosted Komboe server does not contact Komboe. It also does not contact Bitwarden, GitHub, Cloudflare, or the websites in the vault unless you turn that on. Desktop and CLI update checks stay off until you point them at a feed you host. Have I Been Pwned is contacted only when you start a check yourself: opening the exposed-passwords report or pressing the breach-check button on a login. Each check sends only a k-anonymity hash prefix, never the password. The web vault uses the server it was loaded from. Until you save a server URL, the browser extension, desktop, CLI and Android apps default to the upstream Bitwarden cloud servers. Set your server URL when you first sign in. See the switches in docs/RUNNING.md.
