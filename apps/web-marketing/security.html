Security & zero-knowledge

We designed Komboe so that we can't see your data — even if we wanted to.

How Komboe protects your vault

🧮

Encryptoglyph encryption

Every vault item is encrypted on your device with Encryptoglyph before it is stored or synced.

🗝️

Master password stays local

Your master password and derived keys never leave your device and are never sent to our servers.

☁️

Ciphertext-only servers

Our servers store encrypted blobs. A breach of the server alone does not reveal your vault contents.

🚫

No backdoor

No escrow key, no hidden recovery path, no exceptions.

🔁

No admin password reset

Neither Komboe nor an organization admin can reset your master password. Losing it and your recovery kit means losing access — that's the point.

🔍

Open source

Komboe's source code is public on GitHub so anyone can review how it works.

One account, every device

One Komboe account, one sign-in, on every device. You sign in with your email and master password (optionally a passkey and 2FA). Your master password is used on your device to derive the keys that decrypt your vault, so it is never sent to our servers.

Accounts & sign-in FAQ

What is a Komboe account?

One Komboe account syncs and protects all your devices and apps: the web vault, browser extensions, Android, desktop, CLI and the MCP agent integration. You sign in once with your email and master password, optionally with a passkey and two-factor authentication.

Does Komboe ever see my master password?

No. Komboe is zero-knowledge: your master password never leaves your device and your vault is encrypted before it syncs.

Can an admin reset my master password?

No. There is no backdoor and no admin password reset. Keep your recovery kit safe.

Does this website ask for my password?

No. Log in and sign up happen in the Komboe web vault, never on these marketing pages.

What about self-hosting?

Self-hosted servers use local Komboe accounts by default. Organizations can optionally add enterprise SSO via OIDC or SAML for sign-in; vault decryption still requires each user's master password.
