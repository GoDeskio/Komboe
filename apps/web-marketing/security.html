Security & zero-knowledge

We designed Komboe so that we can't see your data — even if we wanted to.

How Komboe protects your vault

🧮

Encryptoglyph encryption

Every vault item is encrypted on your device with Encryptoglyph before it is stored or synced.

🗝️

Master password stays local

Your master password and derived keys never leave your device and are never sent to GoDesk.

☁️

Ciphertext-only servers

Our servers store encrypted blobs. A breach of the server alone does not reveal your vault contents.

🚫

No backdoor

No escrow key, no hidden recovery path, no exceptions.

🔁

No admin password reset

Neither GoDesk nor an organization admin can reset your master password. Losing it and your recovery kit means losing access — that's the point.

🔍

Open source

Komboe's source code is public on GitHub so anyone can review how it works.

Account vs. vault

You sign in with your Komboe account (one account works across our other apps). That proves who you are. Your vault is then unlocked separately with your master password, which only you know. Compromising your account login does not decrypt your vault.

Accounts & sign-in FAQ

Do I need to set up any identity server?

No. Sign-in is hosted by us at komboe.com and just works — you never need to set up any identity server.

How can I sign up?

Click Sign up and you'll continue on the secure Komboe sign-in page, where you can use email and password, Google, or a passkey. You never type a password on komboe.com.

Does my account work in other apps?

Yes. Your Komboe account works across our other apps too.

Can Komboe or an admin see or reset my master password?

No. Your master password never leaves your device. Komboe is zero-knowledge, and there is no admin reset.

What about self-hosting?

Self-hosted servers can use plain local accounts or bring their own identity provider — Keycloak, Okta, or any OIDC provider. No specific provider is required.
